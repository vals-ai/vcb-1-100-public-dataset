Let a collection owner opt in to a public, read-only share page through a copyable link; existing and new collections stay private until explicitly shared, and each collection reuses its stable link when sharing is re-enabled. A public page shows that collection’s name and live source posts, but never private notes, other collection names, or controls that change the owner’s data.
